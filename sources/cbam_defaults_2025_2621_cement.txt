2.4 million characters of country-by-country default values; this file keeps the cement
tables the transition layer cites and nothing else.

WHY THE FALLBACK TABLE IS THE ONE CITED. Turkiye is the largest single origin of EU
cement imports, and every cement line in its table reads "-". Annex I's own rule says a
listed country with no value takes the figure from "Other Countries and Territories", so
the fallback table IS the operative default for the dominant origin, not a residual.
