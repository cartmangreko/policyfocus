Method and limits

The unit is the duty, not the provision. One provision can carry several duties, and one duty can sit across several provisions.

Named and not named. A sector is named when the text addresses it directly. It is reached without being named when the duty lands on a public buyer, a supplier, or an input it depends on.

Burden drivers. Seven yes-or-no marks read off the text, shown as the strip on each row: D1 new document, D2 accredited outside verification, D3 approval needed before you proceed, D4 new measurement or data systems, D5 recurring, D6 money attached to failure, D7 you are newly inside the regime altogether.

No euro figures. None are given here. Percentage and tonnage thresholds are quoted because they are operative text, not estimates.

Counts are unweighted. One duty on every recipient of free allowances counts the same as one waiver for small aircraft operators. Adding the number of affected companies is the next step and will change the picture.

Coverage. Enacting articles and annexes of both files. Still outstanding: the Accelerator Act's own Annexes I to IV, which set the product-level low-carbon and origin requirements for steel, concrete, aluminium and vehicles.

Ochre flags mark duties whose substance is settled later, in delegated or implementing acts not yet drafted.
