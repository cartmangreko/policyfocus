Field guide to what Europe builds next

eu|fabric

Follows how Europe’s industry is evolving — the technologies coming out of the lab, the projects that turn them into steel, cement, chemicals and batteries, and the rules and money that decide which ones get built.

01 — The mark
One structure, many threads. What you name it depends on how long you look.
Lattice
Many threads held in a single grid — nothing stands alone.
Circuit
Nodes and routes — something switched on, carrying current.
Loom
Warp over weft, loose ends still out — cloth on the frame, not folded.
02 — Construction

Built on a plain weave. Four warp and four weft threads on a 24-unit module, one 4-unit line weight throughout, over and under alternating at every crossing. Threads run 8 units past the grid, so the cloth reads as assembled rather than finished.
