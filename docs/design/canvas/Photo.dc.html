Photography, under one rule

Press images of things that exist

A photograph on this site is a press image of a real project or technology node. No stock, no generated imagery, no concept illustration. A page with no legitimate image runs without one — which is what the two slots below are doing today.

The treatment

Duotone: shadows to ink, highlights to the sector accent, one wash per transition. Hard crop on the grid, no rounding, no shadow, no gradient over the top. The caption states the fact; the credit sits at the end of the same line in monospace.

The wash is the one place a sector accent covers area rather than drawing a line, and it is why the accents were re-cut clear of claret and pine: a duotone in a hue a reader has learned to read as cost would say something about the plant that nobody meant.

Where an image goes

A sector page carries its lead project's photograph beside the lead block. Home carries the latest status change's photograph. Nothing else does.

What each image must carry

The node it belongs to. A caption that states a fact, not a mood. The credit, in the caption line. A licence note per image, in data/images/LICENSES.md.
