# Design history

Handoffs that have been overruled, kept because the specifications inside them
are still the most precise record of how a component was meant to look.

A document lands here rather than being deleted when a later brief reverses its
direction. Each one carries a banner at the top naming what superseded it. Read
them for values — hex, spacing, type scale, construction — and never for
structure: the pages they describe no longer exist in the shape they describe.

  homepage/   The PolicyFocus homepage, 13 August 2026. Superseded by amendment
              brief 2 §6 and brief 3. Its design tokens survived the port into
              web/app/globals.css almost unchanged and are the reason it is
              worth keeping; its home page — the stats strip, the ticker in the
              chrome, the burden band, the six-item nav — was taken apart by the
              sector-map work, and its "no image assets, ever" ruling is exactly
              what brief 2 §6 reverses.
