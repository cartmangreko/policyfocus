Relief · removed · RPT-09

Third-country group reporting threshold raised to €450 million

Institution European Commission File Omnibus I · COM(2025) 81 Article Art. 2(12), Dir 2013/34/EU Status Adopted · Dir (EU) 2026/470
What changes

Before this amendment, an EU subsidiary of a third-country group had to publish group-level sustainability information once the parent's EU turnover exceeded €150 million; where there was no subsidiary, a branch triggered the duty above €40 million. The Omnibus raises both lines.

The subsidiary threshold moves to €450 million of net EU turnover in each of the last two consecutive financial years, and the branch threshold to €50 million. Third-country groups sitting between the old and new lines lose the disclosure duty entirely.

Prior rule vs new rule
Prior rule
Trigger
Parent EU turnover > €150m (subsidiary) or branch turnover > €40m.
New rule
Trigger
Parent EU turnover > €450m for two consecutive years; branch > €50m.

  • Annual group-level disclosure duty removed for mid-size third-country groups

Costs

Source text

"The first and third subparagraphs shall only apply to the subsidiary undertakings or branches referred to in those subparagraphs where the third-country undertaking, at its group level … generated a net turnover in the Union exceeding EUR 450 000 000 for each of the last two consecutive financial years."
